## Decision register

- **Deployment boundary:** Cloudways does not permit this deployment account to create or rename a sibling release directory beside its assigned `public_html`, so the current host cannot truthfully offer an atomic directory swap. The new clean-app CLI verifies a trusted release and empty database, fingerprints and backs up the target, copies private/public files, restores the webroot on a post-copy failure, and forces log-only mail. CI proves that source path, but it does not run host-specific cache purge, bootstrap or tenant creation inside one atomic transaction. On a new hosted app, activate before opening to users, bootstrap the empty database separately, purge only that app's cache, and audit served files and ledger. A populated-company in-place upgrade needs its own migration-compatibility and rollback design; it must not reuse the clean-install path or claim database rollback from file restoration. Hosted rehearsal and operational cutover are still open.

- One authoritative ledger and one set of source-owned financial documents per organization; standalone does not mean a second copy of that organization's accounting data. The additional Cloudways app/database is a disposable clean-install test with invented data, not a destination for current customer books. A future standalone organization's own database could serve CoreOne through scoped service APIs, but those cross-app contracts remain an explicit integration gate. Existing organizations stay on their canonical CoreFlux/CoreOne database without an approved cutover.
- Standalone Billing's invoice form depends on the shared company directory for client lookup and creation. The exact `modules/people/api/companies.php` script is therefore allowed through the standalone Apache, server-path and API-bootstrap guards, while the broader People module remains denied. - An entity-bound service key may prepare documents and request review only under its explicit scopes; a human reviewer owns approval and posting.
- The AP payment creator cannot release their own draft or queued payment. The shared UI explains this and excludes creator-owned payments from bulk release and draft bank-batch origination; the server remains authoritative. A cleared payment is corrected by a reasoned reversal, not by deleting its posted journal. The source bill reopens and the bank line must be matched again to a replacement payment. This preserves the audit chain across correction and recovery.
- A hard accounting close is allowed only after that period's end date and after every earlier period for the same legal entity is closed or locked. Both checks run under the same entity/period lock as posting and cannot be bypassed by marking checklist tasks complete. An operator may prepare the checklist or soft-close earlier; the month-end screen explains why hard close is unavailable. The existing database-clock `ready_to_close` filter and the new `has_ended` field use the same boundary. This avoids prematurely freezing a current month or letting an earlier open month change balances behind a later close.
- Generated finance PDFs must use a private, per-request temporary directory, not a fixed shared folder that another server user may own. The close packet, money-movement digest and AR statement now share that response helper, which cleans temporary files and keeps renderer paths out of customer-facing errors. - Manual AP discounts are refused until a credit-side treatment, tax behavior, source correction and report tests are defined. A positive `discount` label must never silently debit expense.
- The AP editor must not offer a discount as a new bill-line choice while the backend refuses it. If a PDF extraction surfaces a discount, preserve it visibly but block save with a vendor-credit explanation. Blank untouched rows may be skipped; a partly completed row must not disappear from a saved bill.
- Direct invoice drafts use the canonical Billing service for both standalone CoreAccounting and CoreOne. Untyped negative lines become untaxed discounts; an explicitly typed discount must be negative and non-taxable. Zero-priced informational service lines are allowed alongside a positive invoice, but zero- or negative-net documents cannot be saved or edited into that state. Blank untouched form rows may be skipped; a partly completed row must be corrected or removed. Browser previews round each line before summing, matching the backend calculation.
- An AP bill CSV line must carry positive quantity and unit price at no more than four decimal places. For an amount-only vendor charge, use quantity `1` and the charge as unit price; subtotal and total are derived or checked in preview. One invalid line blocks the entire bill, including when other bills are imported with `skip_invalid`.
- Invoice CSV uses its own signed-line rules: positive quantity and an explicit rate at stored four-decimal precision; explicit subtotal, tax and total amounts must use cents; subtotal must match quantity times rate. An untaxed negative discount line is allowed only within a net-positive invoice. These checks apply to preview and commit and cannot turn one partially invalid invoice into a saved draft.
- Imported invoice CSV lines accept an explicit supported item type; otherwise a negative subtotal becomes `discount` and a nonnegative manual line becomes `other`. A negative line cannot be mislabeled as labor or another positive type. An optional catalog item ID must resolve to an active item in the same workspace; its type and revenue account are inherited when omitted from the line. An explicit or inherited revenue account must be active, postable and revenue-classified. CSV invoices now resolve an existing client-company ID or upsert by name through the same Billing helper as direct invoices; preview does not create a company. Its source-system label survives import, an operator chooses a bank account and posts it through the same Billing journal as a direct receipt, and the deposit can then be applied or refunded under that journal's ownership checks. CSV updates stop once the receipt is posted or bank-linked, even if its entire amount remains unapplied. A later bank line may be manually matched to that existing journal; this is not automatic reconciliation and must not produce a second cash posting.
- An AP draft allocation reserves a bill but is not a disbursement or ledger payment. Only a released and cleared payment reduces the bill and cash. The clean QA check held AP at $12.50 and cash at $25 while the $12.50 payment was draft, blocked release by its creator, and reduced both controls only after the reviewed payment matched an invented bank debit. A matching retry reused journal 10.
- Billing's payment worklist pages and searches on the server by client, reference or external source ID. It reports the full filtered count and caps each page at 200 rows; it must not silently hide later imported receipts. The signed-in search and paging interaction is still part of human acceptance.
- An invoice's incoming receipts panel defaults to reference or exact-outstanding-balance candidates; unrelated recent deposits remain available behind an explicit disclosure but cannot be applied with one click. A weak or oversized candidate opens the exact bank line in entity-scoped reconciliation, and Open bank feed goes to the transaction worklist. This is a review aid, not a matching assertion: final service-side invoice, entity and remaining-balance checks still control posting.
- Receipt candidate search first reserves up to 25 reference or exact-balance matches across all eligible unmatched bank lines, then adds recent deposits and deduplicates before returning a maximum of 25. The priority search and recent search share tenant, bank currency, entity and invoice-date restrictions. The 103-line fixture proves the bounded-result behavior, not performance on a large real bank history; broader load remains open.
