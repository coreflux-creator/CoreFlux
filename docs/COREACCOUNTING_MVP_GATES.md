# CoreAccounting MVP release gates

CoreAccounting is an independent product surface on the **existing CoreFlux/CoreOne accounting core**. Invoices, bills, bank transactions, approvals, subledger links and posted journals remain in their current source modules and shared ledger. This checklist is an acceptance ledger, not a claim of production readiness. Detailed evidence and decisions live in `COREACCOUNTING_COREONE_ALIGNMENT.md`.

## Next acceptance sequence

1. Configure the already-created disposable QA app's private database file, then run the guarded empty-database bootstrap, schema contract and invoice-to-cash/bill-to-payment replay. Never clear or reuse the populated simulation database.
2. Complete a signed-in, two-human browser walkthrough of the everyday invoice, bill, bank and report tasks. Capture concrete friction and fix it before sign-off.
3. Reconcile non-synthetic opening balances, AR/AP source lists, bank statements, entity ownership and historical source links to an approved cutover date.
4. Verify controlled provider delivery to approved test recipients; keep payment initiation and payroll compliance behind separate gates.

## Decision register

- One authoritative ledger and one set of source-owned financial documents per organization; standalone does not mean a second copy of that organization's accounting data. The additional Cloudways app/database is a disposable clean-install test with invented data, not a destination for current customer books. A future standalone organization's own database could serve CoreOne through scoped service APIs, but those cross-app contracts remain an explicit integration gate. Existing organizations stay on their canonical CoreFlux/CoreOne database without an approved cutover.
- CoreAccounting admits the accounting, Billing, AP and Treasury module APIs and four bearer-scoped CoreOne v1 finance contracts. The server-resolved entry-script classifier rejects known unrelated flat APIs and root PHP pages before private database config loads; the CoreFlux ERP retains its existing routes. Direct scripts that never load common config still need webroot packaging or server-level routing, and the finance allowlist needs a full hosted inventory. The finance editors do not offer staffing-only dimensions in standalone mode. A journal already linked to a placement remains readable but cannot be changed there because its assignment context requires CoreFlux verification.
- An entity-bound service key may prepare documents and request review only under its explicit scopes; a human reviewer owns approval and posting.
- Manual AP discounts are refused until a credit-side treatment, tax behavior, source correction and report tests are defined. A positive `discount` label must never silently debit expense.
- The AP editor must not offer a discount as a new bill-line choice while the backend refuses it. If a PDF extraction surfaces a discount, preserve it visibly but block save with a vendor-credit explanation. Blank untouched rows may be skipped; a partly completed row must not disappear from a saved bill.
- Direct invoice drafts use the canonical Billing service for both standalone CoreAccounting and CoreOne. Untyped negative lines become untaxed discounts; an explicitly typed discount must be negative and non-taxable. Zero-priced informational service lines are allowed alongside a positive invoice, but zero- or negative-net documents cannot be saved or edited into that state. Blank untouched form rows may be skipped; a partly completed row must be corrected or removed. Browser previews round each line before summing, matching the backend calculation.
- An AP bill CSV line must carry positive quantity and unit price at no more than four decimal places. For an amount-only vendor charge, use quantity `1` and the charge as unit price; subtotal and total are derived or checked in preview. One invalid line blocks the entire bill, including when other bills are imported with `skip_invalid`.
- Invoice CSV uses its own signed-line rules: positive quantity and an explicit rate at stored four-decimal precision; explicit subtotal, tax and total amounts must use cents; subtotal must match quantity times rate. An untaxed negative discount line is allowed only within a net-positive invoice. These checks apply to preview and commit and cannot turn one partially invalid invoice into a saved draft.
- Imported invoice CSV lines accept an explicit supported item type; otherwise a negative subtotal becomes `discount` and a nonnegative manual line becomes `other`. A negative line cannot be mislabeled as labor or another positive type. An optional catalog item ID must resolve to an active item in the same workspace; its type and revenue account are inherited when omitted from the line. An explicit or inherited revenue account must be active, postable and revenue-classified. CSV invoices now resolve an existing client-company ID or upsert by name through the same Billing helper as direct invoices; preview does not create a company. Ordinary line-level CSV export carries the stored customer, item, type and account identities. Synthetic source-to-collection passed; real bank, delivery and historical acceptance remain open.
- Synthetic staging tests are useful engineering evidence but do not replace real-history reconciliation or human operational acceptance.
- New customer invoice links expire after 90 days. An authorized billing sender can disable every active link for an invoice with an audited reason, or create a replacement link for manual sharing. A successful resend issues a fresh link, disables earlier links and preserves an invoice's paid/partially paid status; a failed provider response disables only its newly issued link. Migration `022_invoice_token_hash_only.sql` drops stored raw tokens and enforces a unique verifier hash without invalidating previously emailed URLs. The shared Billing GET returns link state, never the raw secret; MailService redacts this invoice URL from its persisted outbox body while sending the original message. The LogDriver records metadata but not bodies. This SQL migration requires a coordinated release on a populated ERP, since old Billing code reads the removed column. Provider acceptance is still not transactional with the database update: real-provider retries and competing sends/revokes, plus an outbox decision, remain release gates before live standalone delivery.

- Bank receipt invoice selection loads at most 100 open candidates at a time and states when more exist. Search by customer or invoice number queries the server, retaining a selected invoice in the split picker even if later searches exclude it. The API enforces tenant, entity, currency, date, posted-journal and open-balance filters before returning choices; posting still revalidates the selection. The signed-in browser interaction remains to be checked.
- Concurrent receipt acceptance uses two separately signed-in synthetic operators, not a shared browser session. Under a held bank-line lock, identical split requests must yield one posted journal and one replay with the same two payments. Under a held invoice lock, two distinct deposits of the full balance must yield one posted receipt and one untouched bank line. This is a narrow staging race test, not a real-feed or load-test claim.
- An imported customer receipt is not cash merely because its CSV row exists. Its source-system label survives import, an operator chooses a bank account and posts it through the same Billing journal as a direct receipt, and the deposit can then be applied or refunded under that journal's ownership checks. CSV updates stop once the receipt is posted or bank-linked, even if its entire amount remains unapplied. A later bank line may be manually matched to that existing journal; this is not automatic reconciliation and must not produce a second cash posting.
- Billing's payment worklist pages and searches on the server by client, reference or external source ID. It reports the full filtered count and caps each page at 200 rows; it must not silently hide later imported receipts. The signed-in search and paging interaction is still part of human acceptance.
- An invoice's incoming receipts panel defaults to reference or exact-outstanding-balance candidates; unrelated recent deposits remain available behind an explicit disclosure but cannot be applied with one click. A weak or oversized candidate opens the exact bank line in entity-scoped reconciliation, and Open bank feed goes to the transaction worklist. This is a review aid, not a matching assertion: final service-side invoice, entity and remaining-balance checks still control posting.
- Receipt candidate search first reserves up to 25 reference or exact-balance matches across all eligible unmatched bank lines, then adds recent deposits and deduplicates before returning a maximum of 25. The priority search and recent search share tenant, bank currency, entity and invoice-date restrictions. The 103-line fixture proves the bounded-result behavior, not performance on a large real bank history; broader load remains open.
