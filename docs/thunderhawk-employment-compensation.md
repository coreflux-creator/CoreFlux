# Employment compensation history and payroll dates

Installed and accepted November 14, 2026 in the isolated pilot. Application `c8efdda2` preserves the preceding job-reference release. This work concerns native employment compensation; provider assignment rates, approved placement earnings and the standalone Arabella release remain separate. [Installed evidence](../deploy/thunderhawk-compensation-acceptance-20261114.json).

The previous compensation endpoint authenticated users without enforcing the People read/write grants. Its predecessor update and new-row insert were separate writes, used different tenant-resolution paths, and referenced an `updated_at` column absent from the canonical compensation schema. A new rate could fail after an earlier operation, and the payroll reader treated the previous rate's recorded final day as excluded.

The corrected native endpoint requires `people.view` for history and `people.manage` for changes. Reads resolve the current People directory and require an existing employee in that directory. They return the history and its revision. A new version requires that revision and explicit rate type, integer cents, frequency, currency and first effective day. Optional ending dates are inclusive. Invalid or overlapping history and backdated changes require separate review instead of silently choosing or replacing a rate.

The writer locks the current directory policy, employment record, compensation history and retry receipt. It closes an open predecessor on the day before the new rate, inserts the new version and writes both required audits in one transaction. An exact retry by the same actor and workspace returns its original receipt, including after a subsequent rate was added. Changed retries and stale history are rejected. Caller-supplied actor IDs are refused. The native payroll reader now includes the prior rate's final day and rejects overlapping applicable rows.

Eleven compensation fields have explicit native meanings and ownership in the dictionary. There is no implemented dated-compensation profile receiver in JobDiva, Quanta, PurePay or PurePayroll; the dictionary retains those as explicit gaps. Existing provider earnings or placement-rate packets do not establish employment-compensation synchronization. These definitions neither publish rates nor enable execution.

The shared MariaDB acceptance fixture covers shared and isolated People directories, current scope changes, whole-cent/date validation, same-date conflicts, exact historical retries, inclusion of the final day, overlapping legacy rows, mandatory-audit failure and insertion failure after predecessor closure. It rolls back all synthetic records. Independent connections exercise committed exact replay, conflicting writes and rollback recovery. Native login/API cases cover anonymous and ungranted denial, reader write denial, displayed revision, audited creation, replay, stale refusal, newest-first history and foreign tenant pins.
