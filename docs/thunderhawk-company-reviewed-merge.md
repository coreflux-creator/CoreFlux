# Reviewed company consolidation

The company merge now has a native preview, explicit field choices, a required audit receipt, and safe retry. The old ID-only merge entry point refuses requests. This is source work; it has not been installed in the isolated pilot. Remaining acceptance gates below still prevent treating the complete merge workflow or the broader integration as finished.

## Behavior

- Both records must be active canonical companies in the current Companies directory, with permanent CO numbers. The preview is bound to the reviewer, workspace, installation, current sharing policies, original records, references, source bindings, custom definitions and observed values. An intervening change requires another review.
- Staff choose the surviving value for each ordinary company field, including the name. Roles may come from either company or be explicitly combined. Visible, editable, non-private custom fields can be selected separately. Contacts and each address kind have an explicit primary selection. Changing a choice clears the browser confirmation.
- Migration 199 makes company names unique among active rows. Choosing the retired company's name does not rename its historical record. Active-name uniqueness and migration replay are tested on the native schema; an incomplete schema refuses the new preview.
- All original CO numbers and provider mapping rows remain intact. Number aliases point to the survivor. The retired profile, role history, protected tax values, agreement/document pointers and restricted custom values remain on their original record. Selected company fields are held in Core against later source refreshes.
- Current company references in vendor/client profiles, jobs, placements, client chains, corporate details, economic parties, referrals, contacts and addresses move to the survivor. V and CL identities are not merged or renumbered. AP, Placements, Companies and the other module owners are resolved independently. Inconsistent foreign current references refuse the operation.
- Bills, payments, 1099 history, invoices, journal lines, liability statement lines and approval actor identities retain their original company references. Consolidation does not grant approval authority, execute payroll, pay a bill or post a journal.
- All changes, including primary-contact ownership and the final audit receipt, share a transaction/savepoint. Required audit failure rolls back the operation. Exact retries return the original receipt even after later edits; changed choices cannot reuse that review.
- Company source-list and reverse-lookup APIs now resolve number aliases in the current Companies directory while reading only the authenticated workspace's provider mappings. They require People and integration read access. They omit raw source payloads and error text. Multiple surviving IDs in one provider require a selection rather than an arbitrary forward match.
- The JobDiva job writer holds company identity locks through saving the job. Native address creation locks and rechecks its parent, scopes primary-address demotion to the directory owner, and requires an atomic audit. These close identified write gaps; they do not constitute the remaining full concurrent-writer audit.
