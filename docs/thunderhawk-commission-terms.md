# Commission currency and payment timing

## Installed behavior

Commission rules now retain their own currency, payment frequency and due month. A flat commission needs an explicit currency and frequency; a one-time amount also needs a month in `YYYY-MM` form. A recurring amount cannot carry a one-time due month. Percentage commissions use the calculation currency and earned-time basis. Unknown historical terms remain null and require review. The system does not infer a day of the month or reinterpret INR as USD.

The terms pass through native placement creation and editing, JobDiva's default writers, configured field mappings, CSV import/export, reviewed source replacement, economic parties and newly approved rate snapshots. The existing CSV export still selects one latest commission row per role; it is not a complete multi-row commission-history export. Existing approved snapshots remain unchanged. The placement screen displays separate currency totals and supports editing the terms. Core ownership of a rule set continues to protect it from source refreshes.

Native Time settlement claims an eligible one-time commission once across recipient runs, and a recurring fixed commission once per recipient run across rate batches. The final writer serializes claims with the placement lock and rejects conflicting amounts, stale recipients, retired parties and another tenant's placement. After a nonvoid obligation exists, economic terms and recipient identity cannot be rewritten or deleted through the native/source rule paths. Notes and an end date can still change. Native US payroll continues to accept USD only; this change adds no foreign-currency conversion, scheduled commission payment or provider execution.

The user's confirmed Puneet amount remains INR 7,500, one time, due August 2026. The recipient's full Core identity is unresolved. This release created no real commission rule or obligation for that person.
