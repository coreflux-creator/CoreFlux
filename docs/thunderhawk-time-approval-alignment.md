# Native time approval evidence

Individual-entry approval and the shared weekly approval plan/application service now resolve their evidence under the protected original-workspace transaction. This is source preparation for the isolated Thunderhawk pilot. It has not been installed and does not authorize production cutover or real payroll, payment or financial posting.

## Approval behavior

- The original time workspace stays pinned independently of the caller's selected workspace. Explicit-workspace internal calls must already have authority, such as a validated approval token. They do not change the ambient request tenant or grant new access.
- People, placement and accounting ownership are read and locked afresh. The worker, current placement, work date, original period, weekly header, daily hours, custom category and downstream history must remain eligible.
- The authenticated account's current person association and the entry's preparer are checked for independent review. Weekly worker identity is checked too. External weekly decisions require an email actor different from the worker and preparer and retain that email rather than inventing a user ID.
- Approved rates are resolved in the protected transaction, with deterministic effective-date/ID selection and a locking read. The entry retains its rate ID and records a fingerprint of that rate together with canonical accounting dimensions and their hash. Raw rate values are not added to time-view responses. The fingerprint records approval evidence; downstream comparison against it remains separate unfinished work.
- The legal employer must be the assigned entity in the current accounting directory. A retired or foreign assigned entity cannot be silently replaced by the first active entity. Canonical client/vendor company references must remain available and unmerged.
- A weekly plan includes the complete current entry/header revision and each pending entry's resolved evidence. Application regenerates and compares that whole plan before writing and after Quanta source verification. Missing entries, substituted evidence or changed time, rates or dimensions require a new plan. Independently approved siblings keep their source and approval fields.
- Completing a week verifies current Quanta source evidence, including its already-approved current siblings. Approval writes, required decision receipts, weekly projections and artifacts commit together; failures roll them back, including under an outer transaction. The common weekly service requires its final header receipt as well as each entry receipt.
