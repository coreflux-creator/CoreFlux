The implementation preserves every previously recorded adapter mapping and leaves unknown future fields unreviewed.

These counts are field decisions, not successful deliveries. The preflight separately verified 342 installed runtime fingerprints and all 35,040 original Core rows; no business record was changed by the inventory.
