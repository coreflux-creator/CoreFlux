QuickBooks integration

Disconnect QuickBooks

An authorized CoreFlux tenant administrator can disconnect the tenant's QuickBooks company at any time.

How to disconnect

  1. Sign in to the CoreFlux tenant that owns the connection.
  2. Open Admin → Integrations → QuickBooks Online.
  3. Select Disconnect and confirm the action.

Disconnecting disables future QuickBooks API access and scheduled synchronization for that tenant. It does not automatically delete accounting records previously synchronized into CoreFlux, because the customer may need those records for accounting, audit, or legal-retention purposes.

Data deletion

To request deletion of synchronized QuickBooks data after disconnecting, email support@corefluxapp.com. We verify the requester's authority and apply legal, security, backup-expiration, and financial-record retention requirements described in our Privacy Policy.

If you also want to remove CoreFlux from within Intuit, review connected apps in your QuickBooks account and revoke access there.
