QuickBooks integration

Connect or reconnect QuickBooks

Authorized CoreFlux tenant administrators can connect a QuickBooks Online company using Intuit OAuth 2.0.

Your Intuit password is never shared with CoreFlux. Intuit displays the requested permissions and sends CoreFlux encrypted OAuth credentials only after you approve access.

Before you connect

  • Sign in to the CoreFlux tenant that should own the QuickBooks connection.
  • Use a CoreFlux tenant administrator or master administrator account.
  • Use an Intuit user authorized to approve the selected QuickBooks company.
  • Review the Accounting and, when enabled, Payments permissions on Intuit's consent screen.

What happens next

After authorization, CoreFlux returns you to the QuickBooks connection page, verifies the company, and displays the granted scopes. Synchronization directions remain off until an administrator explicitly enables them.

Ready to continue

Sign in to CoreFlux and open the tenant's QuickBooks integration settings. Select Connect QuickBooks or Reconnect QuickBooks.

Continue to CoreFlux

For help, contact support@corefluxapp.com. You can review our Privacy Policy and Terms and EULA before connecting.
