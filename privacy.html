Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 23, 2026

This policy explains how CoreFlux collects, uses, shares, protects, retains, and deletes information when organizations and their authorized users use the CoreFlux platform, website, and integrations.

1. Scope and roles

CoreFlux ("CoreFlux," "we," "us," or "our") provides multi-tenant business operations, accounting, billing, staffing, reporting, and integration software. An organization that subscribes to CoreFlux generally controls the business data entered by its users. CoreFlux processes that data to provide the service and acts as a service provider or processor where applicable.

2. Information we collect

Account and organization information

We collect account names, business contact details, authentication and access-control information, tenant and entity settings, support communications, and subscription or service administration information.

Business and financial information

At the direction of our customers, we process accounting records, chart-of-account data, customers, vendors, invoices, bills, payments, journal entries, documents, staffing and time records, reports, and related audit information.

QuickBooks information

When an authorized user connects QuickBooks, we receive OAuth access and refresh tokens, the QuickBooks company identifier, granted scopes, connection status, and the QuickBooks records the customer chooses to synchronize. Depending on enabled settings, these records may include company, account, customer, vendor, invoice, bill, payment, and transaction data.

Payment information

For QuickBooks Payments, card or bank details are sent directly from the customer-facing browser to Intuit for tokenization. CoreFlux does not receive or store the raw primary account number, security code, full bank-account number, or full routing number. CoreFlux receives an opaque Intuit token and may store transaction identifiers, status, amount, currency, card brand and last four digits, or bank-account metadata returned in masked form.

Usage, device, and security information

We collect service logs, IP addresses, browser and device information, timestamps, security events, error reports, and audit trails needed to operate, secure, diagnose, and improve the service.

3. How we use information

  • Provide, maintain, secure, and support the CoreFlux service.
  • Authenticate users and enforce tenant, entity, role, and approval controls.
  • Synchronize data and perform customer-requested actions with QuickBooks and other connected services.
  • Process or track payments through the payment processor selected by the customer.
  • Generate reports, exports, audit trails, and operational insights requested by users.
  • Prevent abuse, investigate incidents, comply with law, and enforce our agreements.
  • Communicate about the service, security, support, and material policy changes.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use QuickBooks data for cross-context behavioral advertising or provide it to data brokers or advertising networks.

4. How we share information

We share information only as needed with authorized users in the customer's tenant; connected services such as Intuit when a user directs an integration action; infrastructure, email, monitoring, security, and professional-service providers bound to appropriate confidentiality and data-protection obligations; a successor in a merger, financing, acquisition, or sale of assets; or government authorities and other parties when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, and service integrity.

5. QuickBooks connection and disconnection

Only an authorized CoreFlux administrator can connect a QuickBooks company. The consent screen identifies the Intuit permissions requested. A tenant administrator can disconnect QuickBooks from the CoreFlux integration settings. Disconnecting revokes or disables future API access and stops scheduled synchronization for that connection.

Disconnecting does not automatically erase business records already synchronized into CoreFlux, because customers may need them for accounting, audit, contractual, or legal-retention purposes. The customer can request deletion as described below, subject to legal and security exceptions.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain information for the period needed to provide the service, meet customer instructions, maintain financial and security audit trails, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and satisfy legal obligations. Retention periods vary by record type and customer configuration.

Authorized customers may request access, correction, export, disconnection, or deletion by emailing support@corefluxapp.com. We verify authority before acting. We may retain limited information when required by law, needed to establish or defend legal claims, or preserved in security logs and backups until normal expiration.

7. Security

CoreFlux uses administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, including encrypted HTTPS transport, encryption of QuickBooks OAuth credentials at rest, tenant scoping, role-based access controls, approval workflows, audit logging, secure secret handling, monitoring, and restricted administrative access. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

We use session cookies and similar technologies needed for sign-in, security, tenant selection, preferences, and reliable operation. We may use limited service analytics to understand performance and errors. We do not use QuickBooks data for advertising cookies.

9. International processing

CoreFlux is hosted in the United States. Information may be processed in the United States and other locations where approved service providers operate, subject to applicable contractual and legal safeguards.

10. Children's privacy

CoreFlux is a business service and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or obtain a copy of personal information, or to object to certain processing. Business users should ordinarily submit requests through their organization. You may also contact us directly, and we will respond as required by applicable law.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect service, legal, or operational changes. We will post the revised policy with a new effective date and provide additional notice when required.

13. Contact

Questions, privacy requests, and data-deletion requests may be sent to support@corefluxapp.com or mailed to CoreFlux, 2200 Whilden Ct, Charlotte, NC 28211, United States.
